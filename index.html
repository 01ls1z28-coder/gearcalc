Math & sources

  • Speed: mph = RPM × D × π ÷ (gear × final drive × 1056) × (1 − slip). 1056 = 63 360 in per mile ÷ 60 min per hour, both exact. km/h = mph × 1.609344 (exact).
  • RPM at speed: RPM = mph × gear × final drive × 1056 ÷ (π × D) ÷ (1 − slip).
  • Shift landing: landing RPM = redline × next ratio ÷ current ratio; drop = redline − landing.
  • Tire diameter: metric = rim + 2 × section × aspect ÷ 100 ÷ 25.4 (inch = 25.4 mm exactly). Flotation = the first number. Revs per mile = 63 360 ÷ (π × D).
  • Diameter used: the unloaded, nominal diameter by default. A loaded tire rolls on a slightly smaller effective radius, so real speed at a given RPM is a little lower. Enter a rolling factor or a measured loaded radius if you have one. GearCalc does not assume a value.
  • Slip: a torque converter that isn't locked lets the engine spin faster than the input shaft. GearCalc models this as a flat percentage you enter.
